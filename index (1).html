When temptation hits

Stop what you're doing. Breathe in on "Lord Jesus Christ, Son of God" — breathe out on "have mercy on me, a sinner." Tap once per repetition and keep praying slowly until the wave passes. The urge is temporary; make the prayer louder than it.
